# MARS: Colony Builder – User Manual

Nov 15, 2026 · Yasin · Version v1.46

In the game you fly by rocket from a base on Earth to Mars, build a colony, survive and explore. This manual covers every key, place, building and system in the game.

## Goal-based guide: what do you want to do?

This part works as "to do this, do these steps". Find what you want and follow the steps in order. The detailed tables are in the numbered sections further down.

### To survive

**To eat when you are hungry:**

1. If you have 10 or more food in stock, press G: +25 hunger.
2. To fill up more, enter the tent or the shuttle cabin and press G: packaged food +40.
3. With no stock, go to a food capsule and press E: +25.
4. To build up stock, grow crops (see "To grow crops on Mars") or buy 5 packs of food for 12 gold at the shop on Earth.

**To drink when you are thirsty:**

1. If you have 10 or more water in stock, press H: +30.
2. In the tent or shuttle, H: bottled water +45.
3. E at a water capsule: +30.
4. To raise your water stock, press E on blue ice crystals (10–14 water).

**To refill stamina:** Stop running and stand still. Fastest is waiting in the tent or rocket cabin (5×), or sitting in the rest seat.

**To restore health:**

1. Keep hunger and thirst above 20; health refills on its own.
2. Stay in the tent or rocket cabin for faster recovery.
3. E at a habitat: +20 health, full stamina.
4. Sleep in a bed (E): health and stamina refill quickly.

**To sleep:** Go to the bed in the tent, the wall-mounted sleep pod in the rocket or the bed in the shuttle and press E. Press E or Space to wake up.

**To shelter from a radiation wave:**

1. When the warning appears in the DSN bubble, you have 30 seconds.
2. Go into the tent, the rocket cabin or next to a habitat.
3. When the wave ends, "Radiation wave passed" appears; you can go outside.
4. For a lasting fix, upgrade your suit: at level 6 you are fully protected.

**To measure radiation:** Craft a Geiger counter at the workbench (14 metal + 8 copper + 1 capacitor). The current dose rate appears at the bottom left.

**To get through a dust storm:** Get into a shelter or stay near your buildings. Check your buildings afterwards and press E on any damaged one (6 metal).

**To continue after dying:** Press "Respawn". You come back at the nearest safe spot with 60 health.

| Copper | Press E on orange crystals. |

**To find buried resources:**

1. Hold the pickaxe (1) or the shovel (2).
2. Press X: the nearest buried resource in range comes to the surface.
3. To see where they are, hold the scanner (3) and press X; faint dots appear on the minimap.
4. Leave the rover (V) in "keep exploring" mode; it digs them up on its own as well.

**To carry more when storage is full:** Build storage (+50 metal/water/food per level), build a crate (+20 bag), upgrade the bag at the bench (20/40/70 gold).

### To get tools and items

**To get tools for free:** Enter the rocket cabin and press E at the Mars field tool kit at the back left. Any pickaxe, shovel, scanner, soil meter or hoe you do not have is given to you for free.

**To hold a tool:** Press its number key: 1 pickaxe, 2 shovel, 3 scanner, 4 soil meter, 5 hoe. Press the same key again to put it away.

**To craft extra tools or items:** First build a workbench on Mars (67 metal + 45 water + 46 gold), press E next to it and choose what you want.

**To upgrade your space suit:** Choose the suit upgrade at the bench. From level 4 on you need integrated circuits, so collect gold and copper and craft circuits first. Full costs are in Section 9.

**To buy supplies, water and seedlings:** Return to Earth and press E in front of the shop west of the base. Remember to take the gift pack on your first visit.

### To build and improve buildings

**To place a building:**

1. Be on Mars and have built the workbench.
2. Press E at the bench → choose a building from the build menu.
3. Look at an empty spot; click when the ghost building turns green.
4. If it is red, either the spot is taken or you lack resources: look elsewhere or gather more.

**To unlock new features:** Collect science. All buildings are available from the start; science thresholds unlock upgrades: 10 faster mining, 22 better panels, 35 stronger habitats, 50 faster rover.

**To upgrade a building:** Look at it and press U. Level 2: 28 metal + 4 science. Level 3: 50 metal + 10 science.

**To repair a damaged building:** Walk up to it and press E (6 metal).

**To remove a building:** Look at it and press F; 60% of the cost is refunded.

**To send a signal to Earth:** Build an antenna and press E next to it (25 energy).

### To grow crops on Mars

**To find fertile soil:**

1. Hold the soil meter (4).
2. The soil analyzer opens at the bottom right. As you walk, the fertility, pH, perchlorate, nitrate and water of the spot you stand on update instantly.
3. At 75% fertility and above it shows "Very fertile". The best spots are crater floors (old lake beds) and areas near ice deposits; the ground right next to the rocket is poorer.
4. The "Suggested" line at the bottom of the analyzer shows crops that suit that soil.
5. Press X to log the reading for that spot on screen.

**To dig a plant bed:** On fertile ground hold the shovel (2) and press X. If no buried resource is nearby, a bed opens in front of you (4 metal). The bed keeps the fertility of the spot where it was dug. Up to 12 beds, at least 7.6 m from the rocket.

**To hoe the soil:** Hold the hoe (5) and press X next to the bed. The soil is aerated and compost is mixed in; growth is ×1.25 for that crop. Hoe again after each harvest.

**To plant a seedling:**

1. Buy seedlings at the shop on Earth (2 per purchase).
2. Press E on an empty bed and choose a seedling from the list.

**To make crops grow faster:**

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Watering (E, 5 water) | ×2.5 while wet; ×0.5 while dry. Wetness lasts 70 s. |
| Soil fertility | Between ×0.55 (very poor) and ×1.45 (very fertile) |
| Hoe | ×1.25 |
| Dust storm | ×0.7 (less light) |

In the best case (watered, very fertile, hoed) a radish is ready in about 1.5 minutes; in the worst case it takes more than 15 minutes.

**To follow growth:** Stand next to the bed. The hint line shows the percentage, the stage (seed, sprout, leafing, flowering, ripe), a real day counter such as "Day 12/27" and the bed's fertility.

**To harvest:** When a yellow light appears above the plant, press E. You get food + 1 science; sometimes a seedling comes back.

**Which crop should I pick?** Times are real sowing-to-harvest days from NASA experiments; in the game 1 day = 15 seconds.

| Crop | Real time | Yield | Seedlings (2) | Soil need | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Radish | 27 days | 8 food | 3 gold | Very low | APH-02, ISS 2020 |
| Mizuna mustard | 28 days | 8 | 4 | Low | VEG-03, ISS 2017 |
| Lettuce | 33 days | 10 | 4 | Medium | Veggie, ISS 2015 |
| Chinese cabbage | 33 days | 10 | 4 | Medium | ISS 2017, Mir Svet |
| Arugula | 35 days | 8 | 4 | Very low | Wageningen Mars regolith, 2016 |
| Kale | 50 days | 13 | 5 | Medium | Veggie, ISS 2019 |
| Pea | 60 days | 14 | 5 | Low | Wageningen Mars regolith |
| Dwarf wheat | 70 days | 20 | 6 | High | APH, ISS 2018 |
| Potato | 90 days | 26 | 5 | Medium | CIP + NASA, 2016 |
| Dwarf tomato | 104 days | 18 | 6 | High | Veg-05, ISS 2023 |
| Hatch chile | 120 days | 14 | 7 | High | PH-04, ISS 2021 |

**Tip:** Start with radish and arugula on poor soil. Once you find a fertile crater floor, plant wheat, tomato and chile; hoe and water often.

### To travel by rocket and fix engine failures

**To go to Mars:** On Earth, enter the rocket on the pad with E, then press E at the launch capsule. It is free.

**To return to Earth:**

1. Save up 60 metal and 30 energy.
2. None of the rocket parts may be broken (check with E at the telemetry rack).
3. The engine must not be faulty.
4. Enter the cabin and press E at the launch capsule.

**To repair an engine failure:**

1. When the "ENGINE FAULT" warning appears, enter the rocket cabin. Outside, a red light flashes on the rocket and smoke rises.
2. Go to the metal-plated maintenance deck on the left wall (red LED flashing) and press E.
3. The covers open. Drag the yellow, green, purple and red wires on the left with the mouse (or your finger) to the terminal of the same colour on the right.
4. If you drop a wire on the wrong terminal, the panel shakes and the wire snaps back; try again.
5. When all four wires are connected, "ENGINE REPAIRED" appears and the panel closes. The rocket can launch again.

Esc closes the panel early; the fault remains.

**To repair a broken rocket part:** Craft a repair kit and use it at the repair kit rack in the cabin (+40%), or craft a spare part at the bench and fit it.

**To go to the space station:** At the Earth base, press E at the shuttle airlock in the east room. The first flight needs 65 metal + 36 copper.

### To complete missions, earn science and grow the colony

**To finish missions quickly:** Check the next item in the list on the left and find the matching heading in this guide. When a pack is finished, the next one opens.

**To earn science:**

1. Take a sample with E at a sample site on Mars (the rocket carries 6 tubes).
2. Take the samples to Earth and press E at the bench in the base lab: +3 science each.
3. Build a lab; it produces science continuously.
4. Harvest crops (+1) and look through the station window (+2).

**To improve your tools:** In the Earth lab, every 2 samples improve a tool: rock cores the pickaxe, regolith the shovel, atmosphere tubes the scanner.

**To grow the colony:** Build habitats and keep oxygen and food stocks high. When conditions are right, new colonists arrive over time.

**To command the rover:** Press V to switch between "follow me" and "keep exploring". While exploring it finds buried resources, takes panoramas (+2 science) and flies a helicopter (+3 science).

### To choose a difficulty and follow the story

**To choose the difficulty:** Press Play in the main menu and pick one of the three levels. The choice is stored with your save.

| Level | Hazards | Hunger / thirst / stamina |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | No radiation, dust storms or marsquakes | No hunger, thirst at half speed |
| Normal | Moderate storms and radiation | Balanced (hunger 5 days, thirst 2 days) |
| Hard | More frequent, longer and more damaging storms; more frequent and stronger radiation | Realistic (hunger ~1.5 days, thirst 1 day; stamina drains fast) |

On Easy there is no radiation or marsquakes, so a few missions that depend on them cannot be completed.

**To follow the story:** After choosing a difficulty, the intro story begins. Click, or press Space or Enter, for the next line; press "Skip" or Esc to skip it. During play, subtitles appear at the bottom of the screen when you land, when you find each ore type for the first time and when the engine fails.

## 1. Getting started

**Opening the game:** Open `MARS_Colony_Builder_v1.46.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. For multiplayer, open the server's `https://` address (see Section 15).

**Language:** A language screen appears at start: Türkçe, English, Español, 한국어, Deutsch. Your choice is saved; you can change it later under Settings → Sound and Display → Language.

**Main menu:**

- **Play:** Choose a difficulty, watch the short intro story and launch animation (skip with Esc, Space or a click), and a new game starts.
- **Continue:** Loads your last save. Greyed out if there is no save.
- **Multiplayer (4 players):** Connect with a server address, a name and a room code.
- **Settings:** Sound, display, graphics, keys, camera and radio settings.

**Starting state:** You start inside the Earth base with 130 metal, 40 copper and 90 energy, enough for rocket fuel and the space shuttle. Houston sends its first message in the bubble at the top right.

**Suggested order for the first 10 minutes:**

1. Leave the base through the south door (E), go to the supply shop to the west and take the **gift pack**: 3 packs of food, 3 bottles of water, 2 radish and 2 lettuce seedlings.
2. Board the rocket on the launch pad (E) and fly to Mars from the launch capsule. Your rover lands with you.
3. On Mars, collect metal, copper, iron, gold and ice (E).
4. Build the **Workbench + tent**: 67 metal, 45 water, 46 gold. Open the inventory (I), choose "Workbench + tent", look at an empty spot and click.
5. At the bench (E), craft a pickaxe and shovel (or take them free from the rocket's field kit); build water and food capsules.
6. Dig a plant bed with the shovel, plant a seedling and water it.
7. After minute 13, radiation waves begin: when the warning comes, shelter in the tent or rocket cabin.

## 4. Survival and health

On Mars hunger and thirst drop over time; if either reaches zero you die. On Earth, during the rocket trip and on the shuttle they do not drop.

| Stat | How it drops | How it refills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hunger | From 100 to 0 in 5 game days; 1.4× faster while running | G: food from stock +25 (uses 10 food), packaged food +40, food capsule +25 |
| Thirst | From 100 to 0 in 2 game days; 1.4× faster while running | H: water from stock +30 (uses 10 water), bottled water +45, water capsule +30 |
| Stamina | Running, jumping (5), digging and collecting (1) | On its own while resting; 5× faster in the tent and rocket cabin; sleep and seats |
| Health | Radiation, hunger/thirst | Slowly while hunger and thirst are above 20; faster in shelter; sleep, habitat (+20) |
| Oxygen | Only outdoors on Mars; more colonists and storms increase use | Habitat, greenhouse, ice processor, water/food capsules |

These rates are for Normal difficulty; see "To choose a difficulty" for Easy and Hard.

- A warning appears when hunger or thirst drops below 25.
- When stamina runs out you cannot run for a while (until it refills to 15).
- If oxygen runs out, a colonist is lost.
- Packaged food and bottled water can only be consumed in the tent or the shuttle cabin.

**Health panel (Q):** event dose (SPE), career dose (NASA limit 600 mSv), dose rate, heart rate (rises when running), body temperature (drops outdoors on Martian nights), blood oxygen, blood pressure and stamina.

**Blood pressure and speed:** Each time the radiation event dose passes a threshold (10, 30, 60 mSv), blood pressure drops one step, and each step slows you by 8%. To recover, lie in a bed and sleep for 1 game hour; time runs 20× faster meanwhile and you cannot wake until blood pressure is stable.

**Sleep (E, in a bed):** The tent bed, the wall-mounted sleep pod in the rocket or the shuttle bed. Stamina and health refill quickly while asleep; wake with E or Space. During a rocket trip you first strap yourself to the cabin wall with E.

**Death:** The cause of death is shown on screen. "Respawn" brings you back at the nearest safe spot (next to the rocket, tent or a habitat) with 60 health; the game continues where it was.

## 5. Resources, collecting and digging

Walk up to the crystal-shaped ores on Mars and press E to collect. Each collection uses 1 stamina and can be repeated until the ore runs out.

| Resource | Colour | Per collection | Storage limit | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metal | Grey | 8–12 (8–16 with faster mining) | 100 + storage | Almost everything |
| Copper | Orange | 8–12 | 65 | Circuit parts, buildings, shuttle fuel |
| Iron | Dark red | 8–12 | 50 | Capsules, circuit parts, suit |
| Rare metal | Yellow | 14–18 metal + 70% of that as gold | 100 + storage | Gold: bench, shop, bag, suit |
| Ice | Blue | 10–14 water | 100 + storage | Drinking, watering, oxygen |

**Buried resources:** About 70% of resources are underground and invisible. There are three ways to find them:

1. **Digging (X):** Hold the pickaxe (1) or shovel (2) and press X. The nearest buried resource in range comes to the surface. The pickaxe reaches 2.4 m, the shovel 3.2 m; the shovel digs faster.
2. **Scanner (3 + X):** Tells you how many buried resources are nearby and shows them as faint dots on the minimap.
3. **Rover:** Roams on its own and brings buried resources to the surface (Section 12).

If you dig with the shovel and no buried resource is nearby, a plant bed opens in front of you (Section 8).

**New ores:** A new ore appears at random on Mars every 12 seconds (up to 10; when full, the oldest disappears).

**Lab improvements:** Every 2 Mars samples analysed in the base lab on Earth raise a tool by one level (up to 3): rock cores improve the pickaxe (faster, longer reach), regolith the shovel, atmosphere tubes the scanner (wider scan).

## 8. Mars farming

You buy seedlings at the supply shop on Earth and plant them in beds you dig with the shovel on Mars. The plants are species actually grown in NASA experiments.

There are 11 crops, and their times are based on real sowing-to-harvest days. For the full list, soil fertility, the hoe and growth multipliers, see "To grow crops on Mars" above.

**Planting steps:**

1. Buy seedlings at the shop on Earth (2 per purchase). They appear in the inventory (I).
2. On Mars, hold the shovel (2) and press X. If no buried resource is nearby, a plant bed opens in front of you (4 metal). Up to 12 beds, at least 7.6 m from the rocket.
3. Look at an empty bed and press E, then pick the seedling to plant.
4. To water, press E on the bed: uses 5 water. Wet soil makes the plant grow 2.5× faster for 70 seconds. Water again when it dries.
5. When the plant is fully grown, a light appears above it. Harvest with E.

**Harvest reward:** Food + 1 science. A seedling comes back with a 35–50% chance (always for potatoes).

**Tips:** Start with radish and lettuce, switch to potatoes when you have gold to spare. Lining beds up next to the greenhouse makes watering easier. Radiation waves do not harm plants.

## 9. Radiation and hazards

The biggest danger on Mars is radiation waves from the Sun (SPE, solar particle event). When you see the warning, run to a shelter.

**Radiation wave:**

- The first wave can arrive after minute 13 of the game, then every 17–60 game minutes.
- A DSN warning arrives 30 seconds before the wave.
- During the wave, an aurora appears in the sky and wavefronts spread across the screen.
- **Safe places:** rocket cabin, tent, habitat, shuttle and Earth.
- If caught outside, you lose health and build up dose. Your suit's protection reduces this damage.
- Radiation can also damage rocket parts by up to 35% (Section 10).

**Dose:** On Mars you receive a constant 0.64 mSv/day, and 1.8 mSv/day during the rocket trip. If your career dose goes over NASA's 600 mSv limit, a warning appears and health drops faster.

**Geiger counter:** 14 metal + 8 copper + 1 capacitor at the workbench. Shows the current dose rate (mSv/h) and counts per minute (CPM) at the bottom left; the clicks speed up as the dose rises.

| Dose rate | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Below 0.05 mSv/h | Normal |
| 0.05–5 mSv/h | Elevated |
| 5 mSv/h and above | Danger |

**Space suit upgrades (workbench):**

| Level | Protection | Cost | Extra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0% | Starting suit | — |
| 2 | 15% | 30 metal + 10 copper | — |
| 3 | 32% | 40 metal + 15 iron + 5 gold | — |
| 4 | 50% | 55 metal + 20 copper + 10 gold + 1 IC | — |
| 5 | 70% | 70 metal + 25 iron + 15 gold + 2 IC + 10 science | — |
| 6 | 100% | 90 metal + 30 copper + 30 iron + 25 gold + 3 IC + 25 science | Radiation-proof, bag +20 |

As the suit level rises, the colour of the stripes on the character's suit changes.

**Dust storm:** Arrives every 85–135 seconds and lasts 16–28 seconds. Visibility drops, solar panels produce less, oxygen use rises and buildings can be damaged.

**Marsquake:** Every 4–9 minutes. The screen shakes and buildings can be damaged; repair with E.

These timings are for Normal difficulty; on Easy there are no radiation waves, storms or marsquakes, and on Hard they are more frequent and stronger.

## 10. Rocket and travel

The rocket is the only way between Earth and Mars. On Earth it stands on the pad next to the launch tower; on Mars it waits at the first landing site.

**Fuel:** The trip from Earth to Mars is free. Returning from Mars to Earth costs 60 metal + 30 energy.

**Launch steps:**

1. Walk up to the rocket door, press E and enter the cabin.
2. Go to the launch capsule and press E.
3. On Earth the tower arms swing back, the countdown starts and the rocket lifts off.
4. During the trip you can strap into the wall sleep pod with E.
5. On landing on Mars, the rover lands with you. On return to Earth the rocket lands vertically on the pad and the tower arms reattach.

**Rocket cabin:**

| Object | What E does |
| --- | --- |
| Control panels | Status of the rocket's systems |
| Navigation | Route and trip information |
| Launch capsule | Launch |
| Rest seat | Refills stamina |
| Radiation sensor | Current dose and SPE status |
| Telemetry rack | Part health |
| Repair kit rack | Use a repair kit |
| Wall-mounted sleep pod | Sleep |
| Engine maintenance deck | Opens the wire panel when the engine is faulty |
| Mars field tool kit | Free tools (pickaxe, shovel, scanner, soil meter, hoe) |
| Door | Exit |

**Rocket parts and damage:** The rocket has three main parts: hull shield, power cell and guidance unit. Radiation waves damage them, and a damaged part can prevent launch.

- **Repair kit:** Adds 40% health to a part (8 metal + 2 copper).
- **Spare part:** Crafted at the bench, fitted with circuit parts and fully restores the part (Section 6).

**Engine failure:** Around 4 minutes after landing on Mars, and then every 6–11 minutes, the engine can fail. The rocket cannot launch until you fix it at the maintenance deck (see "To travel by rocket and fix engine failures").

**Sample tubes:** The rocket carries 6 tubes. Go to one of the 8 sample sites on Mars and take a sample with E: Berea, Malay, Rochette, Atmosphere tube, Rock core #2, Regolith #2, Atmosphere tube #2, Rock core #3. When you bring them to Earth and have them analysed in the base lab, each sample gives +3 science and every 2 samples improve a tool.

## 11. Earth

There is no hunger, thirst or radiation on Earth; here you shop, have samples analysed and fly to the space station by shuttle.

**Base building (three rooms, door to the south):**

| Room | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| West: lab | Suit pods, lab benches, lab assistant (E: analyses Mars samples) |
| Centre: control | Computers and operator, rocket part racks, south door |
| East: engineering | Engineer, launch control station, comms console, records rack, robots #1 and #2, assembly arm, shuttle airlock |

**Supply shop (west of the base):**

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 5 packs of food | 12 gold |
| 5 bottles of water | 10 gold |
| Gift pack (once) | Free: 3 food + 3 water + 2 radish + 2 lettuce seedlings |
| Seedlings | See the table in "To grow crops on Mars" |

Items go into your bag; the bag holds 30 and can be upgraded at the bench.

**Launch complex:** North-west of the base: the pad, the launch tower and its arms. The rocket always lands here.

**Space shuttle:** Boarded through the airlock in the engineering room. The first flight needs 65 metal + 36 copper of fuel.

- **Inside the shuttle:** bed, galley (food/water), tool kit (gives 1 repair kit every 30 seconds), pilot seats, system panel.
- **Space station:** E at the observation window = +2 science (once). TDRS satellites relay data to the DSN. This visit is one of the "Long term" missions.

## 12. Mars

Mars is where you build your colony: low gravity (38% of Earth's), a thin atmosphere, cold nights and radiation.

**Time:** 3 real seconds = 1 game minute; a day (sol) lasts 72 real minutes. Nights are dark, body temperature drops outdoors and solar panels produce nothing.

**Rover:**

- Waits next to the pad on Earth and lands on Mars with you on your first trip.
- Runs on an MMRTG (nuclear battery), so it never needs charging.
- Roams on its own, uncovers buried resources and reports to the DSN.
- Now and then it takes a panorama (+2 science) or flies its mini helicopter (+3 science).
- **V key:** Switch between "follow me" (it follows you) and "keep exploring".
- Shown as a blue triangle on the minimap.

**Tent (built with the bench):** Bed, eating and drinking, fast stamina refill. A radiation shelter.

**Sample sites:** 8 marked spots on the map (Section 10).

**Shelters:** Rocket cabin, tent and habitats.

## 13. DSN communication and sounds

Earth talks to you through NASA's Deep Space Network (DSN); messages appear in the bubble to the left of the minimap.

**When messages arrive:** when you collect a resource (what the ore is and what it is used for), when you sleep, launch or return to Earth, when you build or craft something, when a mission is completed, and before a radiation wave.

**What the bubble shows:** The speaker (Houston / JPL / Mars Relay), the link path (for example Canberra → MRO → you) and the light delay (3–22 minutes, depending on the distance to Mars). Messages queue up and each one stays for a few seconds.

**Antenna:** If you build an antenna on Mars, press E to spend 25 energy and send a signal to the DSN; this is a mission.

**Sounds:**

- NASA's Quindar beeps play at the start and end of DSN messages.
- With an internet connection, the game plays NASA's publicly released sounds (launch, Martian wind, Juno, Apollo recordings and more); without one it synthesises similar sounds itself.
- On Mars you hear the wind, the rover's motor and Geiger clicks.
- Volume: Settings → Sound and Display → Volume.

## 14. Missions, science and CML

Missions appear in packs in the left panel; when a pack is finished the next one opens, and after the four main packs the game keeps generating new ones.

| Pack | Missions |
| --- | --- |
| Getting started | 8 basic tasks such as building the bench, crafting tools, collecting resources and placing the first buildings |
| Exploration | Rover finds 3 resources, DSN signal from the antenna, 2 solar panels, greenhouse, survive an SPE in shelter, harvest a crop, collect 3 samples |
| Colony | Colony of 4, a level 3 building, CML 4, 60 science, repair the rocket, rover finds 8 resources |
| Long term | Survive 5 sols, get through 3 SPEs under 600 mSv, survive a marsquake, visit the station, lab levels totalling 6, 6 colonists |
| Endless | New mining, building, farming and exploration goals |

**Science:** From the lab, sample analysis (+3), harvests (+1), rover panoramas (+2) and helicopter flights (+3), and the station window (+2). It unlocks upgrades (10 faster mining / 22 better panels / 35 stronger habitat / 50 faster rover) and is spent on upgrades and the suit.

**CML matrix (M):** NASA's Concept Maturity Level table. Each row is a system in the game (energy, life support, communication, farming, radiation protection and so on) and its level rises as you progress. The CML label at the bottom left shows the overall level.

## 15. Multiplayer and radio

Up to 4 people can play in the same room and talk by voice over the radio. One person needs to run the server.

**Server setup (short version):**

1. Install Node.js on the computer.
2. Run `npm start` in the server folder (`sunucu/`; details in `KURULUM.md`).
3. If your friends will connect over the internet, publish the server with HTTPS (for example through a tunnel service). The microphone only works over HTTPS or on localhost.

**Connecting:** Main menu → Multiplayer. Enter the server address, your name and a 4-letter room code. Everyone who enters the same code ends up in the same room. The first person to join becomes Player 1.

**Radio (bottom left):**

- Each player's radio sits at the bottom of the screen with only its top 20% visible.
- When someone talks, their radio rises halfway, lights up and its screen shows "Player 1", "Player 2" and so on.
- **Auto mode:** Transmits whenever you talk; T mutes/unmutes.
- **Push-to-talk mode:** Hold T while you talk.
- Choose the mode: Settings → Sound and Display → Radio mode.
- The browser asks for microphone permission on the first connection; without it you can only listen.

You see the other players as astronauts in the world with their names above their heads.

## 16. Saving, settings and FAQ

**Saving:** Save with R, load with L. The game also saves automatically every 150 seconds. Saves are kept in the browser; clearing browser data deletes them.

**Settings:**

| Tab | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Sound and Display | Volume, brightness, language, radio mode |
| Graphics | Resolution, shadows, particles, FPS counter, detail, view distance, glow, "Maximum quality" |
| Controls | Key bindings, mouse sensitivity, invert Y axis |
| Camera | Starting camera, touch controls, field of view (FOV) |

**Frequently asked questions**

**The game runs slowly.** Lower the resolution and view distance in the Graphics tab, and turn off shadows and glow.

**I can't place a building.** You must be on Mars, the workbench must be built first and the ghost building must be green. If it is red, look elsewhere or collect more resources.

**A plant bed won't open.** You need the shovel in hand, must be at least 7.6 m from the rocket, there must be no buried resource nearby and you need 4 metal. You can have up to 12 beds.

**I can't return to Earth.** You need 60 metal + 30 energy, badly damaged rocket parts must be repaired first and the engine must not be faulty.

**There is no sound.** Click the screen once (browsers block sound until the first interaction) and check the volume.

**I can't hear my friend on the radio.** Both of you must have allowed the microphone and the server must be opened over HTTPS. In push-to-talk mode, remember to hold T.

**I keep dying from radiation.** Craft a Geiger counter, run to a shelter as soon as the warning appears and upgrade your suit; at level 6 you are fully protected.

**I don't know what to do next.** Press K: the in-game guide opens on the topic that fits what you are doing.
